System Capabilities

What ReliefLine Does

A structured pipeline connecting prediction, pre-positioning, monitoring, and validation - end to end.

{{ icon('package', 20, '#5347ce') }}

Pre-positioning Support

Uses predicted demand and current warehouse stock levels to guide inter-warehouse redistribution ahead of forecasted typhoon-related disasters.

{{ icon('check-circle', 20, '#5347ce') }}

Allocation Monitoring

Supports food pack allocation review from warehouse to target barangays, and lets PSWDO and CSWDO/MSWDO users track allocation status.

{{ icon('clipboard', 20, '#5347ce') }}

Distribution Validation

Supports photo-based or signature-based validation records to confirm that food packs reached their intended barangay recipients.

{{ icon('trending-up', 20, '#5347ce') }}

Predictive Food Pack Allocation

Uses a Linear Regression model to estimate recommended food pack quantities per barangay, based on population, poverty incidence, disaster risk, calamity history, and prior allocations.

{{ icon('building', 20, '#5347ce') }}

Warehouse Stock Monitoring

Monitors food pack inventory and non-food items such as hygiene kits and kitchen kits across provincial warehouses.

{{ icon('map', 20, '#5347ce') }}

Geospatial Mapping

Displays barangay-level predicted food pack demand and priority status using interactive maps.

About ReliefLine

From Fragmented to Data-Driven

ReliefLine transforms fragmented relief planning into a centralized, data-driven workflow — from barangay-level reporting to provincial warehouse management.

Built as a capstone research project aligned with the Province of Pangasinan's disaster preparedness goals, it uses Linear Regression to estimate food pack demand at the barangay level based on historical disaster and allocation data.

  • {{ icon('check-circle', 16, '#1e8449') }} Evidence-based food pack allocation using a predictive model
  • {{ icon('check-circle', 16, '#1e8449') }} Centralized warehouse stock visibility for PSWDO

Privacy Notice

ReliefLine is a restricted-access system used by the Provincial Social Welfare and Development Office (PSWDO), City/Municipal Social Welfare and Development Offices (CSWDO/MSWDO), and authorized barangay personnel of the Province of Pangasinan to coordinate typhoon-related relief operations.

What we collect

  • Account information for authorized users - name, email, and office or barangay assignment.
  • Disaster response records submitted through the system - barangay damage reports, relief requests, allocation and distribution records, and warehouse stock levels.
  • Basic system activity logs (who approved or updated a record, and when) kept for accountability.

How it's used

Data in ReliefLine is used solely to plan, coordinate, and monitor food pack allocation, pre-positioning, and distribution during typhoon-related disaster response in the Province of Pangasinan. It is not shared outside the PSWDO, CSWDO/MSWDO, and barangay offices involved in relief operations.

Access

Access is limited to authorized personnel with an assigned account. Every account's actions are logged for accountability, consistent with the system's role-based design.

{{ icon('file-text', 28, '#0f2547') }}

Terms of Use

By accessing ReliefLine, you agree to use the system only for the authorized disaster-response coordination duties assigned to your role - PSWDO, CSWDO/MSWDO, or barangay-level personnel - in the Province of Pangasinan.

Account use

  • Accounts are personal and must not be shared.
  • Reports submitted through the system - damage assessments, relief requests, and distribution/validation records - must reflect actual, good-faith information.
  • Unauthorized access or use of ReliefLine outside of official relief operations is prohibited.

System availability

ReliefLine requires an active internet connection. It is provided as a decision-support tool - its predictions and recommendations are estimates and do not replace the professional judgment of PSWDO, CSWDO/MSWDO, and barangay personnel.

{{ icon('phone', 28, '#0f2547') }}

Contact Support

For account access, password issues, or technical support with ReliefLine, reach out through your own office's usual chain:

  • Barangay-level users - contact your City/Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO/MSWDO).
  • CSWDO/MSWDO personnel - contact the Provincial Social Welfare and Development Office (PSWDO).
  • PSWDO personnel - contact your ReliefLine System Administrator.

Locked out of your account? Use the Forgot Password? option on the sign-in page to send a reset request to a System Administrator.
