How to read these numbers

Each figure compares forecasts with what actually happened in months the model had not seen (rolling-origin backtest). 12-month total error is how far the full-year figure is off. Stockpile-line coverage is how often real demand stayed at or below the recommended-stockpile line (target 90%). MAE / RMSE / WAPE / R² judge each individual month, which is harsh: no model can know months ahead which week a typhoon will arrive.

Honest reading: on this history the model is about as accurate as the simple guess "same as last year" - it does not beat it. Each season in the history was bigger than the one before, and none of these methods can foresee a growing trend, so all of them under-forecast the newest seasons. What the model adds is the seasonal shape (which months to prepare for), a stockpile line that comes from the data's own spread rather than a guess, and figures that stay consistent with past seasons. Re-check once more real records are loaded.
